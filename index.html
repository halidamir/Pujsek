pujsek-backup-*.json
How to sync between devices

1. On your phone: tap Export backup

2. Save the file to iCloud Drive or AirDrop it to your iPad

3. On your iPad: open Pujsek → Sync → tap Restore → choose the file

4. Choose Merge (keeps both) or Replace (overwrites)
